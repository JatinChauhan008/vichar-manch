Who we are

Vichar Manch – The Nukkad Natak Team

Vichar Manch is a theatre-based campaign and communication team specialising in corporate awareness programmes, workplace safety, social impact initiatives, NGO projects and brand promotions.

With our head office in Delhi and operations in Mumbai, we have a strong Pan-India presence: 150+ trained artists across 150+ cities. Our network includes experienced actors, writers and directors who create performances around each client’s specific requirements.

Our name says what we do — vichar means thought, manch means stage. We put the ideas that matter on a stage where everyone can see them.

Why live theatre

A poster is read in seconds. A play is remembered.

Most workplace communication is one-way: a circular, a slide deck, a banner at the gate. People see it, nod, and forget it.

A nukkad natak is different. The audience stands in a circle, close to the actors. They laugh, they recognise their colleagues and themselves in the characters, and they see the consequence of a bad decision play out in front of them. That emotional connection is what changes behaviour.

  • Reaches every level — from senior managers to contract workers
  • Works in any language and any literacy level
  • Starts conversations that continue after the show

Our approach

Every campaign starts with understanding your objective

  1. Understand

    We learn your objective, audience, site, language and any incident learnings or SOPs.

  2. Develop the concept

    We propose a storyline and characters that match your message and workforce.

  3. Write the script

    A custom script — included in our rate, with no separate script charges.

  4. Rehearse & coordinate

    Artists are cast, rehearsed and scheduled for your locations and shifts.

  5. Perform

    Live on site, followed by audience interaction to reinforce the key points.
