Before you call

Common questions

How long is each play?

Each performance runs about 20 to 30 minutes. We can make it shorter for shift change-overs or add interaction time for a longer session.

Do you need a stage, sound system or equipment?

No. Nukkad natak is performed in the open with live voice and traditional instruments. A clear space at the gate, canteen, shop floor or an open yard is enough.

Which languages do you perform in?

Hindi, English, or the local language of your site, so the audience connects immediately.

Can the play be customised to our site?

Yes. We build scripts around your own SOPs, site rules, incident learnings and terminology so every performance feels like it belongs to your workplace. The script is included in our rate.

Can you cover all our shifts or multiple locations?

Yes. We can schedule several performances across shifts in a day, and with 150+ artists across 150+ cities we can run campaigns at multiple locations across India.

How do we get a quotation?

Call or WhatsApp us, or send the enquiry form with your location, dates, number of shows and topic. We will share our company profile and a quotation.
