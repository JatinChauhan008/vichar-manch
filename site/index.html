How we work

From your brief to a live performance

  1. Understand

    We learn your objective, audience, site, language and any incident learnings or SOPs.

  2. Develop the concept

    We propose a storyline and characters that match your message and workforce.

  3. Write the script

    A custom script — included in our rate, with no separate script charges.

  4. Rehearse & coordinate

    Artists are cast, rehearsed and scheduled for your locations and shifts.

  5. Perform

    Live on site, followed by audience interaction to reinforce the key points.

Planning a safety week or awareness campaign?

Tell us your dates, locations and topic. We’ll suggest the right plays and share a quotation.
