Our service

Plant & Mine Safety Plays

Working at height, confined space, LOTO, vehicle movement, fire safety, housekeeping and PPE — acted out where the risk actually lives.

Call +91 81691 84646WhatsApp us
Overview

Why this works

Safety rules are usually known. What fails is the moment someone decides a shortcut is fine “just this once”. Our safety plays put that moment in front of the workforce — the skipped harness, the bypassed lock, the missing permit — and show what follows, in a language and setting every worker recognises.

Performances happen right on site: at the gate, in the canteen, near the shop floor or in an open yard. No stage, no sound rig, no disruption to operations.

What we cover

  • Working at height
  • Confined space entry
  • Lockout-tagout (LOTO)
  • Mobile equipment & vehicle movement
  • Fire safety & emergency response
  • Housekeeping
  • PPE discipline
  • Permit-to-work

Ideal for

  • National Safety Week
  • Plant safety drives & audits
  • Induction of new workers
  • Post-incident awareness
  • Shift-wise coverage of the full workforce

Every script is customised around your SOPs, site rules and local language — included in our rate.

Process

From brief to performance

  1. Understand

    We learn your objective, audience, site, language and any incident learnings or SOPs.

  2. Develop the concept

    We propose a storyline and characters that match your message and workforce.

  3. Write the script

    A custom script — included in our rate, with no separate script charges.

  4. Rehearse & coordinate

    Artists are cast, rehearsed and scheduled for your locations and shifts.

  5. Perform

    Live on site, followed by audience interaction to reinforce the key points.
